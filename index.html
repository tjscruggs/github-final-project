Prototype Rules

  • Move 1 selected engineer to any adjacent empty tile, then perform one action.
  • Trebuchet components near your castle: Base → Frame → Counterweight → Sling.
  • Sabotage removes one enemy component (never the Base).
  • Siege Action removes one component globally (never the Base), then skips your next turn. 2-turn cooldown.
  • After Sling is complete, you must spend a later turn to Fire and win.
  • Commanders provide era-themed engineer visuals and unique tactical perks.
